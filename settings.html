AI and YouTube connection

Enter the PROXY_ACCESS_TOKEN from your own proxy, not your Claude API key. Saved in this browser for all profiles. Not included in profile exports.

Optional. Use a proxy you own or trust. Recent chat and phrase details are sent there for AI suggestions. AI and YouTube chat are disabled until configured. The same proxy is used for every profile in this browser.

Chrome may offer to save the proxy URL and access token in Google Password Manager when you save. You choose whether to accept.

To disable this connection, clear the URL and press Save proxy. Profile switching and profile imports do not change this setting.
