Guide

Get ready to chat, stream and be you.

1. Set up a profile

Open Profiles, choose a saved profile or create a new one, and enter the Twitch channel you want to follow. Profiles keep different setups ready for different games or streams.

Open profiles →

2. Choose your voice

In Settings, choose a browser voice or set up ElevenLabs. Adjust the voice to suit you. Voice settings are shared across profiles in this browser; use Export voice settings to keep a backup.

Open voice settings →

3. Keep useful phrases ready

Use Phrases to save replies you use often and organise phrases for your games. You can import phrases and export a backup for later.

Open phrases →

4. Open chat and respond

Open Chat to follow your channel. Choose a saved phrase or type your own words, then use the speech controls when you want to speak. To send messages to Twitch chat, connect your Twitch account in Profiles.

You choose what to say, speak or send.

Open chat →

AI suggestions and YouTube

AI suggestions and YouTube chat need a configured proxy connection. Open Settings to add your proxy URL and access token if your proxy requires one.

Open connection settings →
